# Product

## Platform

web

PWA installed on an iPhone through Safari (« Sur l'écran d'accueil »), opened full screen. Mobile web, not native.

## Users

A single user: the owner of the vehicles. They maintain them partly themselves, at home, and partly at a garage. There are no accounts, no sharing and no other audience.

Fleet at launch: **2 to 4 vehicles/machines** in total, mixing car, motorbike and garden machinery (mower, chainsaw).

## Product Purpose

Never lose a vehicle's maintenance history again: what was done, with which part, for what price, and what is left to do. Today invoices, part references and photos are scattered between the photo gallery, emails and paper. You have to dig them out by hand at every service or at resale.

Success: everything is in one place and an entry is quick to make. The key flow (open the app, touch the vehicle, « Nouvel entretien », photograph the part and its reference, enter the price, save) takes **less than a minute**.

## Positioning

A personal maintenance logbook that works fully offline. All data stays on the phone, with no server, no subscription and no account. It handles cars and machines that count in **hours** (mower, chainsaw) in the same place, thanks to a meter unit chosen per vehicle.

## Operating Context

- **At home, hands dirty**: in the garage or workshop, phone set down nearby, greasy fingers or gloves. Entries must be short, with large targets, pre-filled fields and the camera one tap away.
- **From the couch, afterwards**: catching up on entries, reading the history, comparing part prices, preparing a sale.
- Use with no network (garage, garden) must work.
- Documents handled: invoices, part references (manufacturer and equivalents), photos of parts, invoices and before/after condition.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Built (spec steps 1-2, MVP): vehicle list and creation, vehicle page with an editable meter, maintenance form (date, meter, operation type, place: myself or garage, labour cost, notes, photos), history, labour cost for the year.

Planned (spec, in order):
3. Parts catalogue, references, link to maintenance records, manually entered price offers, best price.
4. Reminders by km/hours and/or months; next due date computed from the last maintenance of the same type; red dots on the home screen.
5. ZIP export/import (JSON + photos) as the protection against data loss.
6. Finishing touches: costs per year, search, dark theme, Settings screen (export/import, units, theme).

**Confirmed (2026-10-06)**: support **full invoices** (invoice photo + total amount) in addition to individual parts.

Technical constraints:
- Svelte 5 + Vite + vite-plugin-pwa; IndexedDB via Dexie (7-table schema in `src/db.js`).
- Photos stored as blobs, resized to ~1600 px, JPEG quality 0.8.
- Camera opened through `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture>`.
- iOS can clear local storage: request `navigator.storage.persist()` and remind the user to export every month.
- iOS PWA push notifications are limited: use dots and an « À faire » screen instead.
- Hosted over HTTPS (GitHub Pages or Netlify), required for the service worker.

Out of scope: accounts, sharing, multi-device sync, automatic price fetching, App Store, push notifications.

Undecided:
- Reminder shortly *before* the mileage/hours due (not chosen for now; reminders when due are enough).
- Final app name and icon (« Suivi d'entretien » / « Entretien » are provisional).

Ideas for later: cost per km, part barcode scanning, invoice OCR, sync, PDF logbook for resale.

## Product Principles

1. **One-handed entry in under a minute**: any field that can be pre-filled is pre-filled, and the camera comes before typing.
2. **Data comes first**: nothing is lost; export stays visible and is encouraged, because the phone is the only copy.
3. **One logbook for every engine**: km or hours, car or chainsaw, the same flow.
4. **Usable before complete**: add nothing until the current step is used for real.
5. **Readable afterwards**: the history and the prices must be clear enough to back a sale or a purchase decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use with dirty hands or gloves: generous touch targets and minimal text input.
